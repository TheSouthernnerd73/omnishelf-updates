# OmniShelf

**Your game accounts, social logins and more, all in one private vault on your own PC.**

If you have a lot of accounts, you know the problem: sticky notes, text files, and "which Steam account was that one?" OmniShelf puts every login in one locked, searchable place. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. There is no cloud account to make. You choose one master password, and everything is encrypted behind it.

## What you get

- **One vault for everything.** Steam, EA, Epic, Xbox, Rockstar, Facebook, Twitch, Netflix, Gmail and 50+ more, each with its own logo. Add any other site as a custom entry.
- **Made for people with lots of accounts.** Search, sort, filter by category, star your favorites, color-tag accounts, and edit many at once.
- **Steam tools built in.** Click **Steam** to log into that account. Check Steam accounts for VAC, game and community bans. Mark Call of Duty (Ricochet) bans yourself.
- **One click to the login page.** For other sites, click the platform button to open its login page, then copy your password with one more click.
- **The app changes to match what you're using.** Select Steam and the whole app turns Steam blue. Select Xbox and it turns green.
- **Easy import.** Bring in a whole list from a text or CSV file, with a preview and duplicate protection.
- **Protected against mistakes.** Automatic backup copies, a trash that keeps deleted accounts for 30 days, auto-lock when you step away, and streamer mode that hides names and emails.

## Download and install

1. Go to **Releases** on the right side of this page.
2. Download **OmniShelf-Setup.exe**.
3. Double-click it and follow the steps.

Windows may say "unknown publisher" the first time, because the app is not code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## How to use it

1. **Create your vault.** Open OmniShelf and choose a master password. Write it down somewhere safe. If you forget it, nobody can recover your accounts, including the developer.
2. **Add your accounts.** Click **+ Add account**, or click **Import** to load a list from a file.
3. **Find things fast.** Use the search box, the Sort menu, the category menu, and the platform buttons under the search box.
4. **Log in.** On a Steam account, click the Steam button. On other accounts, click the platform button to open the login page. Use **Copy** to copy a password. It clears itself from your clipboard after 30 seconds.
5. **Check Steam bans (optional).** In Settings, paste a free Steam Web API key. Then click **Check bans**. Accounts you open with the Steam button add their profile link automatically.
6. **Keep a backup.** In Settings, click **Save backup file** and keep the file somewhere safe. OmniShelf also keeps its own backup copies.

## Questions

**Is my data safe?** Your vault is encrypted on your PC with a key made from your master password. OmniShelf never sends your passwords anywhere.

**What does it go online for?** Only for Steam ban checks (if you add a key) and to see if a new version is available.

**Does it work on a phone or other PCs?** It runs on Windows PCs. It does not sync between devices. To move accounts, save a backup file and restore it on the other PC.

**What happens if I forget my master password?** Nobody can recover it, so please write it down.

**How do I update?** When a new version is out, a banner appears in the app with a **Download update** button. Run the new installer over the old one. Your accounts are never touched.
